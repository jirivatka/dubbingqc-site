Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version. Dubbing QC runs entirely on your Mac. Your audio, your findings and your reports are never uploaded anywhere. The app has no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

What never leaves your Mac

Every check — missing lines, reverb and pan, sync, clicks, EQ, printmaster comparison and the rest — runs locally on the audio you give it. Markers, PDF reports and review files are written to a folder you choose, on your own disk. Nothing about a session, an episode or a finding is sent to us or to any third party.

Speech recognition

Two checks can optionally use speech recognition — confirming a missing line by its words, and finding original-language speech left in the dub. It is off by default. When switched on, it uses Apple's on-device model only; no server-based recognition is used. macOS's own permission dialog says speech data "will be sent to Apple" — that is Apple's standard wording for every app requesting the permission, not something this app does. A full run with it switched on was measured with every process's network traffic logged, and no connection was made by the app or any speech service during that run.
